Raman Calibration

從「看到光譜」到「相信數據」From “seeing a spectrum” to “trusting the data”

一條拉曼峰的位置，背後同時牽涉 detector pixel、波長校正、雷射波長與光柵繞射。這套教材不只告訴你公式，更帶你看真實資料、找出儀器問題，最後把方法應用到食品樣品。The position of a Raman peak depends on detector pixels, wavelength calibration, laser wavelength, and diffraction by the grating. This unit goes beyond formulas: you will inspect real data, diagnose instrument problems, and apply the method to food samples.

五步學會 Raman CalibrationLearn Raman Calibration in five steps

先知道光譜從哪裡來，再理解校正、建模與驗證，最後才進入應用。這樣學，公式會變成有意義的工具。First learn where a spectrum comes from, then understand calibration, modeling, and validation before moving to applications. This way, formulas become meaningful tools.

🔦
1. 認識拉曼訊號1. Meet the Raman signal知道 laser、sample 與 detector 在做什麼。Understand what the laser, sample, and detector do.
⚙️
2. 理解波長校正2. Understand wavelength calibration從 pixel 建立 wavelength axis。Build a wavelength axis from pixels.
📈
3. 建立校正模型3. Build a calibration model用標準峰建立可靠的轉換關係。Use standard peaks to build a reliable conversion.
🧪
4. 驗證與診斷4. Validate and diagnose檢查偏移、雷射誤差與異常現象。Check offsets, laser errors, and anomalies.
🍱
5. 真實樣品應用5. Apply to real samples把方法帶入食品添加物分析。Apply the method to food-additive analysis.

三個核心教材單元Three core learning units

建議依序完成。每一個單元都對應一種能力：理解、診斷、應用。Complete them in order. Each unit develops one capability: understanding, diagnosis, or application.

01

先懂校正原理Start with calibration principles

從 detector pixel、波長到 Raman shift，理解「為什麼要校正」以及校正曲線如何建立。Move from detector pixels and wavelength to Raman shift, and learn why calibration matters and how a calibration curve is built.

進入：校正原理 →Open: Calibration principles →
02

幫儀器做體檢Give the instrument a checkup

比較兩台儀器，實際判讀波長軸、雷射波長與二階繞射造成的問題，練習像分析人員一樣找原因。Compare two instruments, interpret wavelength axes and laser wavelengths, identify second-order diffraction, and practice reasoning like an analyst.

進入：儀器體檢 →Open: Instrument checkup →
03

挑戰真實食品樣品Take on real food samples

把前面建立的光譜觀念帶到食品添加物，觀察拉曼指紋，連結儀器分析與實際食品分析情境。Bring your spectral understanding to food additives, examine Raman fingerprints, and connect instrument analysis with real food-analysis situations.

進入：食品添加物 →Open: Food additives →
