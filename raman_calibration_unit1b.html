發現三：二階繞射陷阱Finding 3: the second-order diffraction trap

光柵方程式 mλ = d(sinα + sinβ) 對 m = 1、2、3 都成立：波長 435.8 nm 的光在二階（m = 2）會落在一階 871.7 nm 的位置。Im2000 Pro 沒有 order-sorting filter，所以可見光的 Hg 線與磷光體全部以「×2」的假波長混進來，而且比真正的一階 Ar 線強十倍。用寬頻燈校正時最容易把二階線誤認一階——本課反而把兩條乾淨的二階 Hg 線（407.8×2、435.8×2）當作額外錨點，因為它們的「等效波長」一樣準。The grating equation mλ = d(sinα + sinβ) holds for m = 1, 2, and 3: light at 435.8 nm falls at the first-order position of 871.7 nm when it is in second order (m = 2). The Im2000 Pro has no order-sorting filter, so visible Hg lines and phosphor emission all enter as "×2" false wavelengths, and they are ten times stronger than the true first-order Ar lines. When calibrating with a broadband lamp, it is easy to mistake second-order lines for first-order lines - this lesson instead uses two clean second-order Hg lines (407.8×2, 435.8×2) as extra anchors because their "equivalent wavelengths" are equally accurate.

λ(p) = C0 + C1·p + C2·p² + C3·p³

C0 決定軸從哪裡開始，C1 是色散（每個像素幾 nm），C2、C3 是成像的彎曲修正。哪一個係數錯，就留下哪一種指紋——把「報告 − 真實」對真實波長畫出來，形狀直接告訴你病因：C0 determines where the axis starts, C1 is the dispersion (how many nm per pixel), and C2 and C3 are curvature corrections for the imaging system. Therefore, the correct description of this instrument is not "only 2 points, insufficient to determine dispersion," but "it can determine only an offset; dispersion is fundamentally unmeasurable on this channel" - the former means "we did not measure enough," while the latter means "this instrument cannot do it, and we can calculate why."

第一次為什麼錯？日光燈的 Ar 線在 USB4000 上只有幾百到一千多 counts，峰頂是被雜訊啃過的平台，峰位估計散布 ±0.17 nm，而且系統性偏向零。換成 Hg-Ar 黑光燈管（同樣的 Ar 線強 3–7 倍），五條線的偏差立刻收斂到 ±0.08。這正是 Lellinger 等人跨十台儀器研究建議「校正峰 SNR 至少 100」的原因[12]——校正線的品質，比校正線的數量重要。另一個細節：黑光燈那一檔是雷射開著量的（57 k counts，未飽和），所以軸檢查與雷射量測共用同一次曝光，儀器熱漂移一起抵消——這其實是比分兩次量更好的設計；代價是坐在雷射尾巴上的 Ar 794.8 nm 不能用，紅側只剩 811.5 nm 當錨點——所以第三次關掉雷射、光纖直對燈管再量（500/750/1000 ms × 10），把 794.8 加回來、兩側夾住 785，結果 784.61，與前一次一致到 0.01 nm。順帶一提：這次 365 nm 磷光體丘在 750 ms 以上就飽和了，但它和 Ar 線不在同一批像素，對峰位沒有影響——飽和只要不發生在你要用的線上就無妨。Why was the first attempt wrong? The Ar lines from the fluorescent lamp were only a few hundred to just over a thousand counts on the USB4000, and noise had eaten into their flat-topped peaks. The estimated peak positions spread by ±0.17 nm and were systematically biased toward zero. Switching to an Hg-Ar blacklight lamp, whose Ar lines were 3–7 times stronger, immediately reduced the five-line error to ±0.08. This is why Lellinger et al.'s study across ten instruments recommends a calibration-peak SNR of at least 100[12]—the quality of calibration lines matters more than their number. One more detail: the blacklight-lamp run was measured with the laser on (57 k counts, not saturated), so the axis check and laser measurement shared one exposure and instrument thermal drift canceled out—actually a better design than measuring them separately. The cost was that Ar 794.8 nm, sitting on the laser tail, could not be used; only 811.5 nm on the red side remained as an anchor. Therefore, in the third run we turned off the laser and pointed the fiber directly at the lamp (500/750/1000 ms × 10), restored 794.8, and bracketed 785 from both sides. The result, 784.61, agreed with the previous run to 0.01 nm. Incidentally, the 365 nm phosphor hump saturated above 750 ms this time, but it was not in the same group of pixels as the Ar lines and did not affect peak positions—saturation is harmless as long as it does not occur on a line you need.

def locate_lines(x, y, ref_lines, search_nm=2.5, half_nm=0.9):
    # 第 1 段：每條 NIST 線在 ±search_nm 找最大值，取偏移量的「中位數」當粗略偏移stage 1: find the maximum within ±search_nm for each NIST line, and use the median of the offsets as the rough offset
    rough = np.median([x[m][np.argmax(y[m])] - t
                       for t in ref_lines["wavelength_air_nm"]
                       if (m := (x > t - search_nm) & (x < t + search_nm)).sum() >= 5])
    # 第 2 段：以「真值 + 粗略偏移」為中心，±half_nm 做強度加權質心stage 2: center on the true value + rough offset and compute an intensity-weighted centroid within ±half_nm
    out = []
    for t in ref_lines["wavelength_air_nm"]:
        w  = (x > t + rough - half_nm) & (x < t + rough + half_nm)
        yy = y[w] - y[w].min()
        out.append((np.sum(x[w] * yy) / np.sum(yy), t))
    return out

14. 重點整理與練習14. Key takeaways and practice

三大重點Three key points

  1. 位移＝兩個數字的差。ν̃ = 10⁷(1/λL − 1/λ)：波長軸與雷射波長各自都可能錯，而且指紋不同——前者隨波數變小，後者是常數。看實測誤差的斜率就能分辨。Shift = the difference between two numbers.ν̃ = 10⁷(1/λL − 1/λ): the wavelength axis and laser wavelength can each be wrong, and they leave different fingerprints — the former decreases with wavenumber, while the latter is constant. The slope of the measured error distinguishes them.
  2. 兩把尺各司其職。原子燈只校波長軸、分得開兩個來源；拉曼標準物一步到位、但分不開。兩步法（燈 → 矽/ASTM）是業界慣例。Two rulers, two jobs. The atomic lamp calibrates only the wavelength axis and can distinguish the two sources; a Raman standard handles both at once but cannot separate them. The two-step method (lamp → silicon/ASTM) is standard practice in industry.
  3. 校正是地基。20 cm⁻¹ 的錯會讓資料庫把糖辨識成蛋白質、相似度還有 0.79。任何相似度、分類、定量都建立在橫軸正確之上。Calibration is the foundation. An error of 20 cm⁻¹ can make the database identify sugar as a protein, while the similarity is still 0.79. Any similarity score, classification, or quantitation depends on a correct horizontal axis.
